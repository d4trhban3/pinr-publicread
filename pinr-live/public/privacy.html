Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

The short version. PINR has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. We do not collect personal data about you. What you choose on the site stays in your own browser.

What stays on your device

PINR remembers your choices in your browser's own storage: the coins you hide, the rude-name filter, a wallet address you looked up, your tiles-per-row, theme and background settings, and any watch list or alerts you set. These are never sent to us. They are not tied to a name or an email, because there is none. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or with the controls in Settings.

The "Your choices" code in Settings is text made on your device. It holds only your hidden coins, the name filter and a looked-up address, so you can paste it into another browser or the iPhone app. Nothing is sent to us when you copy or paste it.

What our server receives

Like any website, our server receives each request your browser makes: the address asked for, the time, your IP address and your browser's identifier. We use this to deliver the site, keep it running and deal with abuse. Ordinary access records, kept by the web server and the program behind it, are kept for 14 days and then removed. We do not use them to build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share them for advertising.

When you look up a wallet address, that address is part of the request to our server, which reads public balances from public blockchain data. We do not connect it to you. It may appear in the same access records as any other request.

Ad space applications

If you apply for an ad space, our server keeps what you send: the space, your project's name and link, a small copy of your picture, and the contact you give. Only the PINR team reads it, to reply to you. It is not published and not shared. To stop more than a few applications a day from one place, the server keeps a one-way code made from your IP address for that day's count only; the address itself is not kept with your application. To have an application removed, write to the contact on this page.

Other services

Token pictures, prices and charts are fetched by our server, not by your browser, so the services they come from do not see you. The site's fonts, chart library and background video are served from the same address as the page. When you tap a link to somewhere else (a blockchain explorer, a coin's own links), that other site's own policy applies from that point.

The site runs on PINR's own server and reaches you through Cloudflare, which passes the requests above to us on our behalf.

Who this is for

PINR shows information about newly launched crypto coins, some of which have adult or offensive names and pictures. It is meant for adults. It is not directed at children.

What PINR is not

PINR is a research tool. It shows what was read, from where and when. It is not investment advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: contact@example.com.
